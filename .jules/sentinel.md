## 2024-11-23 - URL Sanitizer SSRF/Open Redirect Vulnerability
**Vulnerability:** `isValidUrl` and `sanitizeUrl` functions rely on `.includes('evzone.com')` or unanchored substring matching on the hostname and protocol allowing domains like `attacker-evzone.com` or `evzone.com.attacker.com` and `javascript:alert(1)//evzone.com` to bypass the validation.
**Learning:** Naive `.includes` for URL hostnames and trusting the unconstrained JavaScript `URL` parsing can lead to severe security flaws like Open Redirects and SSRF. Specifically, the JavaScript URL object will parse malformed schemes correctly and ignore the expected behavior of placing `//` after the scheme.
**Prevention:** Always use strict protocol allowlists and strict equality or anchored subdomain matching (e.g., `=== 'domain.com'` or `.endsWith('.domain.com')`) for hostnames. Ensure all components of the URL are strictly validated against allowlists, handle potentially malicious encoded payloads, and strip unprintable characters/whitespaces prior to passing URLs into the `URL` constructor to mitigate edge-case parsing behaviors.
